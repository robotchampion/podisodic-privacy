Privacy Policy

Podisodic Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 16, 2026

Terms of Service · Contact

Podisodic collects and processes limited information to operate the app, deliver features such as playback, discovery, notifications, feedback handling, and optional backend sync, and improve quality, reliability, performance, and user experience.

Information We Collect

  • Analytics on App activity (e.g. screens viewed, app opens)
  • Analytics on podcast, audiobook, topic, and feature metadata (e.g. content type, source screen, result counts, playback actions, and feature usage)
  • Analytics on Technical metadata (e.g. app version, platform, device/app identifiers provided by analytics SDKs)
  • Anonymous app identifiers we generate to operate backend features (for example, an app-specific user ID and install ID)
  • Notification-related device metadata if notifications are enabled (for example, push token, platform, app version, locale, and timezone)
  • Subscription, topic, audiobook, like, and preference data used to support app features (for example, feed URLs, podcast titles, followed topics, saved audiobooks, like state, and notification preference status)
  • Search and discovery metadata, such as query length, filters, result counts, selected result type, and recent searches stored on your device
  • Performance and diagnostics data, such as startup timing, screen navigation timing, artwork cache behavior, feed refresh timing, timeout counts, and cache diagnostics
  • Feedback and feature request message content that you choose to submit to us through the app

Information Stored On Your Device

  • Podcast subscriptions and settings
  • Followed topics, topic settings, and cached topic episode matches
  • Saved audiobooks, audiobook settings, chapter metadata, and audiobook progress
  • Liked episodes, liked podcasts, playlists, queue state, and recent searches
  • Playback history and resume positions
  • Cached episode metadata and artwork references
  • Playlists and downloaded episode file paths
  • Downloaded podcast or audiobook audio files when you choose to download audio for offline playback

Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect in Analytics

  • Message content submitted in feedback text boxes
  • Email addresses, phone numbers, passwords, auth tokens, or secrets in analytics event properties
  • Full search query text where query-length or result-count data is sufficient

How We Use Information

  • To understand feature usage and improve product decisions
  • To diagnose bugs and improve reliability/performance
  • To monitor app stability and lifecycle behavior
  • To provide podcast, audiobook, topic, library, playlist, queue, and playback features
  • To register devices for new-episode notifications and deliver those notifications when enabled
  • To sync subscription, notification, like, topic, audiobook, and discovery state needed for backend-supported features
  • To extract and display podcast support links, social links, show notes, timestamps, and related metadata from public feeds and show notes
  • To receive, review, and respond to feedback and feature requests you send us

Service Providers

We use third-party service providers to operate parts of Podisodic. These providers may process information on our behalf over encrypted connections (HTTPS), including:

  • PostHog for product analytics
  • Supabase for backend storage, sync, edge functions, likes, notifications, topic/audiobook metadata, social-link metadata, diagnostics, and feedback workflows
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notification delivery
  • Resend for forwarding feedback and feature request emails to support

Third-Party Content Sources And Links

Podisodic uses third-party content sources and directories such as podcast RSS feeds, Podcast Index, Apple Podcasts/iTunes metadata, and audiobook sources such as LibriVox or Internet Archive metadata where available. Podcast websites, social links, support links, Apple Podcasts links, Spotify links, Patreon links, email links, and other external links may open third-party services that have their own privacy practices.

Push Notifications

If you enable push notifications, Podisodic may register your device with a push token and related metadata such as platform, app version, locale, and timezone so we can deliver new episode notifications. You can disable notifications through your device settings or in-app notification controls where available.

Feedback And Support Messages

If you submit feedback or a feature request through the app, the message content you provide is stored so we can review it and may also be forwarded to our support inbox. We do not include that message text in analytics event properties.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Information may be processed by our service providers to operate notifications, analytics, backend sync, and support workflows, and is not used for advertising.

Data Retention

We retain information only as long as needed for product improvement, debugging, notification operations, feedback handling, and other operational purposes.

OPML Import And Export

If you import or export subscriptions using OPML, the file is created or processed on your device and then handled through the system file picker or share sheet you choose to use. Imported subscriptions become normal app subscriptions and may be stored locally or synced to backend features such as notifications if those features are enabled.

Sample Content And Test Profiles

If you choose to load sample content or a test profile, Podisodic may add sample subscriptions, topics, audiobooks, playlists, queue items, playback state, or cached metadata to your app so you can explore or test features. These items are treated like other app data while they remain in your library.

Your Choices

If you want to request deletion of data associated with your usage, contact us at: help.podisodic@gmail.com. You can also remove local app data by deleting subscriptions, topics, saved audiobooks, downloads, playlists, queue items, search history, or the app itself from your device.

Children's Privacy

Podisodic is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
