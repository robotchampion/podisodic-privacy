Privacy Policy for Podisodic

Effective date: July 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Podisodic collects, uses, stores, and shares information to operate the app, notifications, listener tips, Podcast Creator accounts, payouts, analytics, and support workflows.

Information We Collect

  • Analytics on App activity (e.g. screens viewed, app opens)
  • Analytics on Podcast metadata (e.g. podcast title, podcast author)
  • Analytics on Technical metadata (e.g. app version, platform, device/app identifiers provided by analytics SDKs)
  • Anonymous app identifiers we generate to operate backend features (for example, an app-specific user ID and install ID)
  • Notification-related device metadata if notifications are enabled (for example, push token, platform, app version, locale, and timezone)
  • Subscription sync data used to support notification features (for example, feed URLs, podcast titles, and notification preference status)
  • Listener tip activity, such as tip amount, currency, product ID, purchase platform, transaction or receipt identifiers, purchase status, refund or reversal status, show/feed, episode target, timestamps, and related verification metadata
  • Optional listener tip message content and optional listener display name, if you choose to provide them
  • Podcast Creator account information, such as Podcast Creator email, display name, claimed podcast/feed, ownership verification status, payout setup status, and Podcast Creator support activity
  • Podcast Creator payout information needed to send payments, including payout method details and masked account information; full bank account details are stored separately in encrypted form when provided
  • Feedback and feature request message content that you choose to submit to us through the app

Information Stored On Your Device

  • Podcast subscriptions and settings
  • Playback history and resume positions
  • Cached episode metadata and artwork references
  • Playlists and downloaded episode file paths
  • Downloaded podcast audio files when you choose to download episodes for offline playback

Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect in Analytics

  • Message content submitted in feedback text boxes is not included in analytics event properties
  • Tip message text or listener display names submitted with tips
  • Full bank account or routing numbers
  • Email addresses, phone numbers, passwords, auth tokens, or secrets in analytics event properties

How We Use Information

  • To understand feature usage and improve product decisions
  • To diagnose bugs and improve reliability and performance
  • To monitor app stability and lifecycle behavior
  • To register devices for new-episode notifications and deliver those notifications when enabled
  • To sync subscription state needed for backend notification features
  • To verify listener tip purchases and record Podcast Creator support activity
  • To calculate platform fees, Podisodic fees, Podcast Creator net amounts, refunds, reversals, and payout eligibility
  • To operate Podcast Creator account, ownership verification, dashboard, and payout workflows
  • To investigate failed purchases, duplicate charges, refunds, chargebacks, fraud, abuse, and other payment support issues
  • To receive, review, and respond to feedback and feature requests you send us

Listener Tips

If you send a tip to a Podcast Creator, we process information needed to show the tip in the app, verify the purchase with Apple or Google, record Podcast Creator support activity, calculate fees and Podcast Creator earnings, and handle refunds, reversals, payment issues, or fraud checks. Tips are optional one-time purchases and do not unlock premium content, subscriptions, or additional access.

Optional Tip Messages And Names

When sending a tip, you may choose to add a short message or display name. These fields are optional. If provided, they are stored with the tip and shown to the Podcast Creator in their dashboard or support activity tools. They are not intended for public display in the app.

Podcast Creator Accounts And Payouts

Podcast Creators may create accounts, claim and verify ownership of podcast feeds, view support activity, and add payout details. Payout information is used to send Podcast Creator earnings and manage payout support. Full bank account and routing numbers, when provided, are stored separately in encrypted form. Normal dashboard and status APIs use masked account information, such as account type and last four digits, rather than returning full bank details.

Service Providers

We use third-party service providers to operate parts of Podisodic, including PostHog for analytics, Supabase for backend storage and sync, Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notification delivery, Resend for support-related emails, and Apple App Store and Google Play for in-app purchases, purchase verification, refunds, and billing support.

Push Notifications

If you enable push notifications, Podisodic may register your device with a push token and related metadata such as platform, app version, locale, and timezone so we can deliver new episode notifications.

Feedback And Support Messages

If you submit feedback or a feature request through the app, the message content you provide may be stored so we can review it and may also be forwarded to our support inbox. We do not include that message text in analytics event properties.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share information with service providers necessary to operate notifications, analytics, backend sync, listener tips, Podcast Creator accounts, payout workflows, purchase verification, refunds, fraud prevention, and support workflows.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to support product improvement, debugging, notification operations, feedback handling, and other operational purposes.

OPML Import And Export

If you import or export subscriptions using OPML, the file is created or processed on your device and then handled through the system file picker or share sheet you choose to use.

Your Choices

For Podcast Creator account, tipping, or payout-related questions, contact: creators@podisodic.com

Children's Privacy

Podisodic is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact
